Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or book a lesson, we collect your name, email address, and any information you provide in your profile. We also collect lesson notes and homework assignments shared during your tutoring sessions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide tutoring services, schedule lessons, track your progress, process payments through Stripe, and communicate with you about your lessons and account.

3. Payment Processing

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. We do not store your credit card information on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL database with row-level security. Only you and your tutor can access your lesson data.

5. Your Rights

You can request to view, update, or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us at ahmedashrafgeith@gmail.com.
