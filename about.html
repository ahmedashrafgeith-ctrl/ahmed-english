Experience & Qualifications

Teaching Background

I have 3+ years of experience teaching English in both online and traditional classroom settings. I hold a 120-hour TEFL certification and have worked with learners from different linguistic and cultural backgrounds.

My lessons focus on practical communication, speaking confidence, pronunciation, grammar, and vocabulary. I adapt my teaching to each learner’s level, goals, and areas for improvement rather than using the same approach for everyone.

I also have a background in Business Management, which allows me to support learners who want to improve their English for professional communication and workplace situations.

A Supportive, No-Judgment Space

I believe there is no single way to learn English. Your goals, level, challenges, and interests should shape your lessons.

My classroom is a supportive, no-judgment space where you can practise, make mistakes, ask questions, and gradually become more confident.
