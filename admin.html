AdSense Publisher & Slots

Paste your Google AdSense publisher & slot IDs, then control where ads appear (or turn zones off). Ad units render automatically in the marked zones across the site.

Found in AdSense › Account › Settings. Leave empty and the ad areas on the site stay hidden until you set this up.

For each zone pick a method: paste your own ad code (AdSense / other network), OR use the managed AdSense slot below. Paste WordPress-style custom HTML/script whether the zone is on or off — custom code always wins.
